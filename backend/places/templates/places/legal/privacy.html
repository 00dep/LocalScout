Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains how LocalScout handles information when you use the website. For privacy questions or requests, email deeproy.space@yahoo.com.

Information we handle

  • Account details such as your username and email address. Passwords are stored as protected password hashes, not readable text.
  • Information you submit about a place, such as its name, address, location, contact details, opening hours, description, menu and photos.
  • Reviews, ratings and saved places linked to your account. Reviews may show your username publicly.
  • Basic session and security information, which may include IP address and browser details to help protect accounts and operate the service.

How information is used and shown

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, display approved place listings, show reviews and saved places, and respond to support requests. Approved business details and photos, and reviews with usernames, can be visible to website visitors. Do not submit passwords, OTP codes, financial details or private information in a listing or review.

Service providers and cookies

LocalScout uses service providers to run parts of the site. Resend is used to send account verification email, Cloudinary may store uploaded images, and Google services may be used for sign-in or maps. The website is hosted by Render. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy practices. The site also uses essential session and security cookies.

Storage, security and your requests

We keep information while it is needed to operate the service, protect it, or meet legal requirements. No online service can promise perfect security. Account or data deletion is not automatic: contact us through the Requests & Reports page. We may ask for information to verify your request and may need to retain some records for security or legal reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will change when we revise it.
